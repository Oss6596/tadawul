    Reading the Ichimoku cloud

    Ichimoku Kinko Hyo ("one-glance equilibrium chart") shows trend, momentum and support/resistance in one view. This app uses the standard 9 / 26 / 52 settings, which you can change under Parameters.

    LineCalculationMeaning
    Tenkan-sen(9-day high + 9-day low) ÷ 2Short-term balance; fast signal line
    Kijun-sen(26-day high + 26-day low) ÷ 2Medium-term balance; support/resistance
    Senkou Span A(Tenkan + Kijun) ÷ 2, shifted 26 days aheadFast edge of the cloud
    Senkou Span B(52-day high + 52-day low) ÷ 2, shifted 26 days aheadSlow edge of the cloud
    Chikou SpanClose, shifted 26 days backConfirms the trend against past prices

    The five rules behind the score

    1. Price vs cloud: above the cloud is bullish, below is bearish, inside means no clear trend.
    2. Tenkan vs Kijun: Tenkan above Kijun is bullish. A fresh cross (▲/▼ on the chart) is a classic entry signal.
    3. Price vs Kijun: closing above the base line shows upward momentum.
    4. Future cloud: Span A above Span B 26 days ahead (green cloud) points to a bullish outlook.
    5. Chikou: today's close above the close 26 sessions ago confirms the trend.

    A total of +3 or more is strong bullish, −3 or less is strong bearish. Signals are strongest when all five agree. A thin cloud is weak support or resistance.
